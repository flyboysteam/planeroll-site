Planeroll Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Nov 13, 2026

Operator: Flyboys, LLC
Privacy Contact: tower@planeroll.app

1. Introduction

Flyboys, LLC (“Planeroll,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Planeroll mobile application, the planeroll.app website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Planeroll is an aircraft identification and collecting application that allows users to record aircraft sightings, maintain personal aircraft collections, and access aircraft-related information and imagery.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we retain it, and the choices and rights available to users.

For questions or privacy requests, contact tower@planeroll.app.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or access a Planeroll account, we may collect or process:

  • Email address
  • Account and authentication identifiers
  • Sign in with Apple information
  • Authentication and session information
  • Subscription and access status
  • Your AI photo-processing consent choice and the date you accepted it

Authentication is provided through Clerk and supported identity providers, including Apple. Clerk stores account email information, and Planeroll associates user records with Clerk identifiers.

You are not required to provide your real name. An identity provider may supply a name or other profile information you choose to share. Sign in with Apple may provide either an email address or an Apple private relay address.

Aircraft Collection Information

When you create a planespot, we may collect:

  • Aircraft manufacturer, model, family, and variant
  • Aircraft operator or airline
  • Aircraft registration or tail number
  • Date and time of the sighting
  • Airport or location information
  • Whether you were on board
  • Photographs you choose to attach
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

This information is associated with your Planeroll account to maintain your personal aircraft collection.

Photographs

You may choose to capture or upload photographs to help identify aircraft, generate aircraft imagery, or preserve a record of your sightings.

Photographs may contain metadata, including location information.

Uploading photographs is optional. You can manually record aircraft without providing photographs or consenting to optional AI photo processing.

Location Information

With your permission, Planeroll may collect your device's location while you use the app to associate a sighting with a place or nearby airport. Coordinates may be precise, depending on your device settings. Planeroll does not request continuous background location tracking.

Location may also be obtained from GPS metadata embedded in photographs you choose to add. Denying device-location permission does not remove location metadata already present in a photograph.

We use available coordinates to suggest nearby airports. You may search for and select another airport. The selected airport and the sighting's coordinates are separate information; choosing another airport does not replace coordinates already collected for that sighting. Coordinates may be saved with your planespot and processed by our hosting and storage providers.

Location access is optional. You can record aircraft without granting device-location permission and can change that permission in device settings. If you do not want to submit photo location metadata, remove it before adding the photograph.

Subscription Information

When you purchase a subscription, we may receive subscription identifiers, entitlement status, purchase and renewal information, and other transaction-related data necessary to provide access.

Apple processes App Store billing. Qonversion manages subscription and entitlement information.

Planeroll does not receive or store your complete payment-card details from App Store purchases.

Website and Communications

If you have signed up to receive Planeroll email updates, we process your email address and signup information to provide the updates you requested.

If you contact support, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, including your email address and message contents.

Email-update subscriptions are separate from Planeroll accounts. You can ask us to stop these updates by contacting tower@planeroll.app or using an unsubscribe option provided in a message. Account deletion does not by itself unsubscribe a separate email signup.

Technical Information

We and our service providers may process technical information necessary for service operation, security, and troubleshooting, such as IP addresses, request metadata, app or device information, error events, and diagnostic events.

Planeroll does not use your aircraft collection or photographs for targeted advertising. Operational diagnostics and subscription reporting help us run and support the Service; they are separate from advertising tracking.

Optional Notifications

If you ask to be notified when aircraft imagery is ready, we process a push notification token, an app-installation identifier, your account and planespot identifiers, and notification request and delivery information. These allow us to send the requested alert to your device, update its delivery address, and handle delivery failures.

We use the Expo Push Service and the device platform's notification service, such as Apple Push Notification service or Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, to deliver alerts. These services process the delivery token, notification content, and routing information. Our imagery-ready alerts contain a generic message and a planespot identifier used to open the relevant record; they do not include your uploaded photographs or exact sighting coordinates.

Notification permission is optional. You can decline an alert or disable Planeroll notifications in your device settings without losing your saved collection. These controls stop notification display; they do not delete your account or erase previously processed notification records. Notification previews may appear on a lock screen according to your device settings.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Maintain personal aircraft collections
  • Identify aircraft from photographs or registration information
  • Generate and display aircraft imagery
  • Associate sightings with dates and locations
  • Manage subscriptions and complimentary access
  • Provide support and respond to requests
  • Deliver requested imagery-ready notifications and email updates
  • Maintain service reliability and security
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

We process information only for legitimate purposes consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Aircraft Imagery

Planeroll uses automated systems and artificial intelligence to assist with aircraft identification and image generation.

When you choose to use photo-based AI features, your photographs and relevant aircraft information may be transmitted to and processed by external AI service providers, including OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes requests to, such as Google Gemini, for aircraft identification or imagery generation.

Before submitting photographs for optional third-party AI processing, Planeroll provides a disclosure and asks for your permission. We remember your choice on your account so the app does not ask again for each submission.

You may decline and record aircraft manually without submitting photographs. To withdraw an earlier consent choice or request deletion of submitted data, contact tower@planeroll.app. Until your request is handled, do not submit additional photographs if you do not want further AI photo processing. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully performed.

AI providers may process and retain submitted information according to the applicable service terms, configuration, and legal requirements. Account deletion does not instantly remove every provider-held copy. We handle deletion requests with relevant providers where required and remain responsible for our obligations under applicable privacy laws. We do not promise that every provider uses the same retention period or data-use settings.

AI-generated imagery may be inaccurate, incomplete, or visually different from the actual aircraft photographed.

Reuse of Generated Imagery

Planeroll may use photographs and aircraft information to create reusable aircraft artwork.

Generated aircraft imagery may be displayed to other Planeroll users when it represents the same aircraft type or configuration.

Original uploaded photographs are not intentionally published or displayed to other Planeroll users.

Shared generated imagery may remain available after the contributing user deletes their account, provided the retained imagery is no longer associated with or identifying that user and retention is otherwise lawful.

5. How We Disclose Information

We disclose information to service providers when necessary to operate Planeroll.

These may include:

  • Clerk: Account authentication and identity management
  • Apple: Authentication, App Store purchases, and iOS push notification delivery
  • Google Gemini and other model providers accessed through OpenRouter: AI-assisted identification, image generation, and related processing
  • OpenRouter: AI model access and routing
  • Cloudflare: Application infrastructure, photo storage and delivery, networking, and operational diagnostics
  • PlanetScale: Database hosting
  • Qonversion: Subscription status, purchase information, and entitlement management
  • Expo: Push notification routing and delivery
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notification delivery on supported Android devices
  • Website form and email services: Processing email signups and requested communications, including Formspree for signups submitted through its forms

We engage service providers to perform the functions described in this Policy and require appropriate safeguards for the information they process on our behalf. Some providers, such as app stores and identity providers, also process information under their own privacy policies. The provider list may change as the Service evolves.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect the security of the Service, or in connection with a lawful business transaction.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-app behavioral advertising.

Original Photo Privacy

Original uploaded photographs are intended for the user's personal collection and associated processing, not public publication.

Original photographs are delivered through URLs that do not require authentication. Anyone who obtains a valid URL may be able to access the photograph until it is removed or the URL stops working. Do not treat the URL as a password-protected sharing link.

Planeroll does not intentionally publish these URLs to other users. This protection relies on the URLs remaining undisclosed rather than on authenticated access controls. Authorized service providers may access photographs as necessary to provide the Service.

6. Legal Bases for Processing

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we process personal information under applicable legal bases, including:

  • Contractual necessity: To provide accounts, collections, subscriptions, and requested services.
  • Consent: For optional AI photo processing, certain location uses, and marketing communications where required.
  • Legitimate interests: To secure, maintain, and improve the reliability of the Service, where permitted.
  • Legal obligations: To comply with applicable laws and lawful requests.

You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to lawful limitations.

7. Data Retention

Planeroll retains inactive accounts and saved aircraft collections indefinitely until the user requests deletion, subject to applicable law.

We do not automatically delete accounts solely because they have been inactive.

Saved planespot records and associated original photographs remain available while the account exists, subject to applicable service and legal limitations.

Other records, including consent choices, notification requests and delivery records, support communications, and subscription information, may be retained as needed to provide the requested service, resolve problems or disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Disabling notifications, signing out, or uninstalling the app does not itself erase these records.

Retention depends on the information's purpose, the account's status, security needs, legal obligations, and applicable provider and backup schedules. Logs and backups may remain after deletion from active systems under the retention criteria above; they are not necessarily erased at the same time.

Abandoned Photo Uploads

Photos may be uploaded for identification before you save a planespot. Removing a photo from an unfinished draft, leaving the Add Plane flow, or closing the app does not necessarily delete a copy already uploaded or stop processing already started.

Unsaved uploads may remain associated with your account until they are removed through account deletion or another deletion request. Contact tower@planeroll.app if you want to request deletion of an uploaded photo without deleting your account. Provider-held copies, logs, and backups are subject to the limitations described in this Policy.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request permanent account deletion in the app's Settings by choosing Delete account and confirming the request. You can also contact tower@planeroll.app for help with deletion or to request deletion of specific personal information.

An accepted request starts a deletion process; it is not confirmation that every copy has already been erased. We process deletion of your Planeroll account, associated planespot records, account-linked original photographs, and authentication account across the relevant services. Processing may take time when a provider is unavailable or a request must be retried. We handle requests within applicable legal timeframes.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or the establishment or defense of legal claims, to the extent permitted by law. Backups and operational logs may retain limited information until their normal expiration. We work with relevant service providers to address deletion requests as required by law; their processing and backup schedules may affect when deletion is complete.

Shared generated aircraft imagery that no longer identifies or remains associated with the contributing user may be retained, subject to applicable law.

Deleting a Planeroll account does not cancel an active subscription billed by Apple. Cancel separately in your Apple subscription settings to stop future renewals. You do not have to wait until a subscription expires to request account deletion.

We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain the reason and any applicable review or appeal options.
